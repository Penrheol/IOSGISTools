Station Verification Tool DEMO

Business of Weather · agency review workflow for observing-station inventories · companion to the IOS GIS Coverage & Gap Analysis Tool

Purpose: send this file to the met service, hydrological service, or other agency that owns the station network. They open it in a browser (no install, no account), go through the list confirming, correcting, or flagging each station, add any stations we haven't identified, then export a reviewed CSV and send it back. Everything runs locally in the browser — no data is uploaded anywhere. It only works with a list you import; it does not come with any station data preloaded.

1. Reviewer information

Recorded once and stamped on every row when you export — you don't need to re-enter it per station.

2. Import station list to review

Accepts a direct "Station Search Results" export from oscar.wmo.int, the standard OSCAR/Surface export columns (Name, WIGOS ID, Type, Elevation (m), Status, GBON, Latitude, Longitude, OSCAR URL), or the simplified export from the GIS Coverage & Gap Analysis Tool (name, lat, lon, class, variable, status, ...). Column names are matched automatically.

Unchecked statuses are dropped from the list, the progress counts, and the export — by default only Operational stations are in scope for review. Nothing is deleted from the source file; re-check a status to bring it back.

How this works

Purpose. This tool supports Phase 4 (Observing-System Inventory) of the IOS Gap Analysis methodology by giving the national agency a direct way to confirm, correct, or flag the station records we've assembled from secondary sources (like WMO OSCAR/Surface) — and to tell us about stations that aren't in those sources at all.

Verdicts. Confirmed = the record is accurate as shown. Corrected = the station exists but one or more fields were wrong (edit the corrected fields that appear). Not found = you could not confirm this station exists or is where the record says. Duplicate = this record is the same physical station as another one in the list.

What happens to your export. The exported CSV contains the original record plus your verdict, any corrections, your notes, and the reviewer/date you entered above. Business of Weather re-imports this into the GIS Coverage & Gap Analysis Tool to update the working station inventory — nothing is auto-published anywhere.

Nothing leaves your computer. This page does not call any server. The only way data moves is if you choose to export a file and send it on yourself.

Status filter. By default only Operational stations are in scope for review, so reviewers aren't asked to verify stations already known to be closed. Everything imported is kept in memory — checking a status back on brings its stations back into the list, progress counts, and export.
